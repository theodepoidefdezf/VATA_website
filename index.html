04 — Approach

How we think.

A

Craft over speed

An app that ships late but feels right beats one that ships fast and feels off. Every detail compounds.

B

Motion as language

Animations are not decoration — they explain, reassure and guide. We treat them as part of the interface, never on top of it.

C

Native first

We default to platform conventions. iOS feels like iOS. Android feels like Android. Users notice when it doesn’t.

D

Honest collaboration

Clear pricing. Clear timelines. We’ll tell you when an idea won’t work — and propose what will.

08 — FAQ

Questions.

How long does it take to build an app?

A focused MVP usually takes 8 to 14 weeks from kickoff to TestFlight / closed beta. Larger products with backend and complex flows run 4 to 6 months. We share a clear timeline before signing anything.

Do you work on iOS, Android, or both?

Both. We can build native iOS, native Android, or cross-platform with React Native or Flutter. We’ll recommend the right approach based on your product, users and budget.

What does a project cost?

Engagements typically start around €25k for a focused MVP and scale based on scope. We send a fixed scope & price proposal after a free 30-minute discovery call.

Do you handle design, code or both?

Both, end-to-end. From wireframes and product strategy to native code and App Store / Play Store deployment. You can also bring your own designs if you prefer.

What happens after launch?

We offer ongoing maintenance, monitoring and iteration plans. Most clients keep us as their long-term mobile team after the initial release.
